Why can’t I stop a stream?proposed

Start, stop and delete check that you own the stream (or admin its group). There is no platform-admin override, so a harmful broadcast can only be stopped by its host or from SRS directly. The design reserves the button for POST /live/api/admin/streams/:id/stop with a required, audited reason; it would emit stream-ended like the owner path.
