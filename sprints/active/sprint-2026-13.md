## Risks

- **L anchor in a 2-week window.** FEAT-080 is the largest single ticket
  committed since the sprints/ convention started. Mitigations are explicit: the
  NL-builder droppable tail (C/B-sanctioned), the deterministic-only enable gate
  (no flaky LLM-judged tests to stabilize), and TASK-062 as a bounded warm-up so
  the anchor gets an uninterrupted run.
- **Keystone spec-shape risk.** Four downstream tickets consume the agent-spec
  format; a wrong shape now means churn across 2027-01/02. Mitigated by the
  scheduled architect glance and by FEAT-081's accept-but-reject-`parallel`
  requirement being visible at design time.
- **Drift-gate ordering.** If BUG-062 slips past FEAT-080's migration, `db:check`
  stays red and cannot vouch for the new `cortex` tables — the dba lane fronts
  the sprint for exactly this reason; escalate immediately if the
  migrate-vs-allowlist call stalls.
- **BUG-060 is enforcement-surface.** The 2026-12 sr review already burned one
  round on this exact route; the mandatory sr review is in the plan, and the
  pinning test must not be removed until the scoped/filtered fix is verified.
- **QA load.** FEAT-080's lifecycle/persistence/persona-parity checks are manual
  (CI test job non-blocking); the standing runtime-debt table also carries six
  items. QA should prioritize committed-ticket verification over debt burn-down
  if squeezed.
